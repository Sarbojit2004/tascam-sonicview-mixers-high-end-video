## Tone

**Blended: Option 2 (Precise & Technical-but-Accessible) as the base register, with Option 3
(Cinematic & Aspirational) cadence on the open and the continuation line.**

The brief's Section 9 is explicit that this audience — chief engineers, systems integrators and
touring FOH professionals — is highly sensitive to hyperbole and sales pressure. Option 2 is the
register that respects that: it states verified figures and lets them carry the claim. But a
vertical reel has roughly three seconds to earn attention, so the hook and the hand-off to Part 2
borrow Option 3's shorter, weightier cadence. Option 1's consultative warmth is held back for the
CTA, where an invitation rather than a statement is the right move.

**Delivery notes for the read**

- Unhurried and level. The music bed is already authoritative; the voice does not need to push.
- Land the numbers — *0.51 milliseconds*, *54-bit*, *64 by 64* — with a small beat before each.
  These are the proof points and the audience is listening for them specifically.
- No upward inflection at line ends. Statements, not pitches.
- The `dp` section is the one emotional moment in the reel. Slow very slightly on
  "If AC drops, a redundant DC input carries the load." Then a real pause before "No reboot."
- "dp" is read as the two letters, *dee-pee*, not "dip".
- "IF-MTR32" is not spoken; it appears on screen only.
