## Tone

**Option 2 (Precise & Technical-but-Accessible) almost undiluted, with Option 3's cadence reserved
for the close-of-series line only.**

This is the densest part of the three: five distinct expansion cards inside 88 seconds. The read
has to do the opposite of what a reel usually does — it has to get *quieter and more exact* as the
information gets denser, and let the score fall back to its ticking pulse underneath. The brief's
Section 10 asks for precisely this ("pull back to minimal, ticking precision when highlighting the
dense technical data of the IF-Series expansion cards"), and the score is already written that
way, so the voice should follow it down rather than fight it.

**Delivery notes for the read**

- Card names are read as written: *I-F, S-T twenty-one-ten*, *I-F, A-E sixteen*, *I-F, A-N sixteen
  out*, *I-F, M-A sixty-four E-X*, *I-F, D-A sixty-four*.
- "ST 2110" is *S-T twenty-one ten*. "AES/EBU" is *A-E-S E-B-U*. "MADI" is a word — *MAH-dee*.
  "DB25" is *D-B twenty-five*.
- Each card gets one flat, declarative sentence. Do not lift into the next card — a small level
  pause between them is what keeps five products from blurring into one.
- The close-of-series line at 01:12.3 is the only place in the whole three-part series where the
  read is allowed to open up. It should land like a full stop, not a trailer tag.

## Note on IF-MA64/BN

The creative brief documents a sixth card — **IF-MA64/BN**, 64 channels of MADI over 75-ohm coaxial
BNC. **The asset repository contains no photograph of it**, so it has no scene of its own and is
not named in this script; stating it over unrelated imagery would be worse than omitting it.

The coaxial-MADI capability itself is not lost. The IF-MA64/EX card carries *both* optical and
coaxial MADI connectors, and its scene shows and names both — so a facility wired for 75-ohm
video-style cabling still sees its case answered at 00:48.5.
